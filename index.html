FAQ / Help Guide

How do I download on my Mobile device?
Ensure both your PC (running this local server) and your Mobile device are connected to the same Wi-Fi network. Open the local IP printed in the console (e.g. http://192.168.x.x:8000/) on your mobile browser.
Where do my downloaded media files go?
Once completed, the local engine serves the package file directly to your web browser. Check your browser's default Downloads folder.
